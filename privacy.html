Privacy policy for Tabula

Effective from version 1.0

Tabula collects no data.

  • Everything you enter in Tabula (transactions, categories, budgets and settings) is stored only on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync or sharing, the app uses your own iCloud. The developer cannot see data in your iCloud.
  • There is no account, no login and no server.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code.
  • Reminders and notifications are local notifications on your phone.
  • When you export or make a backup, you decide where the file goes.
  • If you delete the app or choose "Delete all data", your data is gone. Make a backup first.
